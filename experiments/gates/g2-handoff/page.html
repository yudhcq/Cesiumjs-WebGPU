G-2 gate page (tasks.md T016). It loads a real Rollup bundle (?bundle=…) that was produced by the production alias plugin, and drives one of two backends per page load: ?backend=webgpu (prefetch adapter/device → handoff slot → synchronous new Scene()) or ?backend=webgl2 (empty manifest → the upstream WebGL2 Context). The result is collected by run.mjs through window.__g2.report.
