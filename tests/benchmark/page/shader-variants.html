SH-6 device page (tasks.md T079, hypothesis H-6, budget.json revision 3). It drives the production emitter's output through upstream's [numberOfDayTextures][flags] variant semantics and measures compile counts and cost: a cold-cache startup prewarm of the 36-variant plan, a documented 300-frame session served from that pool (0 compilations), and — reference only, never a criterion — the cold complement of the plan. The spec (tests/benchmark/shader-variants.spec.mjs) collects window.__shaderVariants and judges it against the pre-registered thresholds.

This page renders nothing and reads nothing back. SH-6 is a gate on compile counts and compile cost, not on pixels; the pixel-side gates are separate suites (SH-2/SH-5). The only GPU work here is createShaderModule + createRenderPipeline, both of which are counted and timed.
